TempoSync
Set your delay to the beat, not by ear.
Open the app
At 120 BPM a quarter note is 500 ms, a dotted eighth is 375 ms and an eighth triplet is 166.7 ms.

What it does

  • Every note from 1/1 to 1/32, straight, dotted and triplet, in ms
  • LFO rate in Hz and delay length in samples
  • Bar length for common time signatures
  • Tap tempo and Italian tempo names
Maths is exact. Tempo name ranges are from Wikipedia and overlap on purpose. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
