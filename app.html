TempoSync

Turn a tempo into delay times, LFO rates and bar lengths. Tap it in if you do not know the BPM.
Method: a quarter note lasts 60000 / BPM milliseconds, a whole note four times that, dotted notes are 1.5 times longer, triplets are two thirds. Rate in Hz is 1000 / ms, which is what a tempo-synced LFO or tremolo runs at. Samples are ms times the sample rate / 1000. A bar is its beats times (4 / note value) quarter notes. Tempo names and ranges are from Wikipedia, "Tempo", and overlap on purpose, since historical usage varies. Tap tempo averages up to the last 8 taps and starts over after a 2.5 second pause. Range 20 to 300 BPM. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
